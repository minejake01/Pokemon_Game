# PRD #5: Poke Arena

## Starters
Bulbasaur (1),  Charmander (4),  Squirtle (7)

## Game Described
An open world pokemon game, should have the basic pokemon mechanics such as battling and capturing. Have three types of pokeball: Pokeball(1x), Superball (1.5x), and Ultraball(2x), also biomes, such as swamp, forest, snowy areas. 5 pokemons in a team at a time. 

## Win or lose
Lose: All your Pokemon in your team faints, zero revives left.  
Win: Collect All 151 types of Pokemon

## Server decides
Pokemon Stats, Chance to see each type of Pokemon in certain biomes, Pokemon you have.

## Screens
Title Screen, Main Game Screen, Battle Screen, and Backpack.

## Out of scope
1: Gen 2 or higher pokemons
2: Gen 2 or higher pokemon types (e.g. Dark Type)
